## Phase 4: Governance System Ratified

Rules locked:

- **Fixed Seats (4):** Permanent. Cannot be removed or replaced.
- **Rotating Seats (7):** Serve on rotating basis.
- **Ideas:** Each member brings exactly one per session.
- **Voting:** Simple majority. Tie → revote until supermajority.
- **Elimination:** Lowest vote-getter after 3 consecutive low sessions is removed.
- **Replacement:** Remaining members vote on new persona. Must have real influence.
- **Resignation:** Any rotating member may resign. Replacement voted immediately.
