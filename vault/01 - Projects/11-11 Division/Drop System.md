# Drop System

> Release structure and cadence.
> Source: founding council sessions — "DROP 001 = THE DESCENT" (turn 149).

## Model: Story-Chapters

Each drop is a chapter in the lore. The 11:11 logo stays constant;
only the chapter graphic changes.

| Drop | Theme | Status |
|------|-------|--------|
| Drop 001 | THE DESCENT — fallen guardians, broken halos, torn wings | Pre-production |
| Drop 002 | (planned — further into the loop) | Planned |

## Drop 001 Mechanics

- 15 pcs (MOQ) — scarcity by capital, not marketing
- Seed 5 shirts to ambassadors/breakers
- Tease before drop (mockup-based, no photoshoot)
- Lore-gate content introduces the world
- Cultural safety test before production (3 Manado locals)

## Rules

1. No collabs until the foundation is proven
2. Every drop advances the loop narrative
3. Collection incentive: "complete the loop"
