## The Backbone

> After the heavenly war, one-third were cast out. Some followed darkness.
> Some regretted it. Some descended by choice to protect humanity from temptation.
> All lost their divinity. Broken halos. Torn wings.
>
> These angels now act as silent guardians guiding humans toward redemption
> while allowing them to taste imperfection.
>
> They not only guide — they tempt human nature to taste sin, letting them
> suffer before helping them. They teach without preaching.
> They work in the dark to serve the light.
>
> **Redemption is a loop, not a finish line.**
