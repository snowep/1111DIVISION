# Content Strategy

> How the brand communicates.
> Source: founding council sessions (Instagram strategy, turns 150-160).

## Channels

**Instagram** — @1111division.official (primary, only active channel)

## Bio Rules (User-Locked)

- No brand name in bio (it's in the IG handle)
- No "based in", no vendor info
- Keep it subculture

## Content Pillars (Drop 001)

1. **Lore-gate image** — first image = a gate into the world (coordinates, tease)
2. **Tease content** — before product exists, mockup-based
3. **Product reveal** — mockups, no photoshoot (no budget)
4. **Community** — breakdance scene, Manado, ambassadors

## Post Principles

- Teach without preach — never explain the lore directly
- The design speaks, the caption hints
- Coordinates as location lore (e.g., 1°16'14.716"N 124°51'15.486"E)
- Organic subculture tone, not advertiser tone

## Content Calendar (Drop 001 Sequence)

| Phase | Content |

| Reveal | Back print detail shots (mockup) |
