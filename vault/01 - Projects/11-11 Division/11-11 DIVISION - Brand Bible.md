## Founding Story

The user established the brand with a projected capital of Rp5,000,000 and zero sales.
The brand targets breakdancers in Manado — a community with strong talent but little exposure
(Jakarta gets the attention). The brand exists to:

1. Regenerate young talent in the breakdance community
2. Find a new way to be closer with God (street x religion, subtle, never explicit)
3. Work in the dark to serve the light
4. Teach without preaching

The founding council (Supreme Council, 11 seats) shaped the brand across 215 conversation turns:
Steve Jobs (product/ecosystem), Virgil Abloh (semiotics/street-luxury), Shawn Stüssy
(authenticity/organic growth), G-Dragon (avant-garde/K-pop), plus rotating temporary members.

## Design System

### Primary Brand Symbol — 11:11 (front/chest)

- The 11:11 forms a subtle **cross**
- Left 11 and right 11 have a subtle differentiation
- Opening and closing marks were refined smaller after council review
- Used on the chest: **mid-chest placement** (council discussed mid vs left)

### Drop 001 Back Print — Final Approved Spec

```
SYMBOL:
• Circle (10cm diameter)
• Crosshair (vertical + horizontal line through center)
• Break the circle at 45 degrees (imperfection)
• Line weight: 3pt bold

TEXT BLOCK (below symbol):
• Font: Plus Jakarta Sans Mono (or Regular if Mono unavailable)
• Size: 8pt for specs, 14pt for brand name
• Color: White (#FFFFFF)
• Content:
    11:11 DIVISION
    UNIT: 001      STATUS: WAITING
    ORIGIN: MANADO FUNCTION: OBSERVER
    LOOP: OPEN     TIMECODE: 11:11
    DESIGNED FOR THE UNNOTICED DETAILS

PLACEMENT:
• Center back
• Top of symbol: 10cm below collar
• Total height: ~25cm
```

### Eye + Clock Symbol (Drop 001 foundation imagery)

- Single abstract minimal eye
- **Pupil = clock face with hands at 11:11**
- No triangle, no rays, no secondary clock
- Line weight: 2–3pt minimum (screen-printable)
- Size: A3 max, centered
- Color: White ink on black tee
- Culturally neutral; **must pass Manado cultural safety test**

### Typography

- **Logo typeface:** Söhne (user chose to stick with Söhne using the free font; alternative: Plus Jakarta Sans)
- **Back print text:** Plus Jakarta Sans Mono, 8pt specs / 14pt brand name

### Color Palette

- Base: black tees (primary)
- Print: white ink
- (Palette expansions discussed but black/white is the founding system)

## Lore — The Story Backbone

> The brand's narrative foundation (user-provided, council-refined).

> "We write the lore to integrate it into the brand bible — historical guidance,
> but subtle." — User, Turn 44 area

**Story Backbone (user's original):**

> After the heavenly war, one-third were cast out. Some followed darkness.
> Some regretted it. Some descended by choice to protect humanity from temptation.
> All lost their divinity. Broken halos. Torn wings.
> These angels now act as silent guardians guiding humans toward redemption
> while allowing them to taste imperfection.
> They not only guide — they tempt human nature to taste sin, letting them
> suffer before helping them.
> They teach without preaching. They work in the dark to serve the light.
> **Redemption is a loop, not a finish line.**

**DROP 001 = THE DESCENT** — the first drop tells the descent story.

## Key Decisions Locked

1. **DROP 001 = THE DESCENT** (story focus for the first drop)
2. **Approved back print:** single eye, pupil = clock at 11:11, no triangle/rays/noise
3. **Cultural safety:** show to 3 Manado locals to test occult association before production
4. **Lore card** explaining the eye/watch/loop meaning accompanies the product
5. **11:11 logo remains primary brand symbol (front)**
6. **Silhouette:** photograph ambassador (B won 6-3-0), DIY Rp0-200k
7. **Typography:** stick with Söhne (free font) / Plus Jakarta Sans Mono for back text
8. **Pricing:** max Rp249k, unified range 239k–249k
9. **No explicit religious imagery** — culturally neutral, Indonesian streetwear safe
10. **Tease** before drop; content without photoshoot (mockup-based)
