# 11:11 DIVISION — Design System

> The visual language of the brand, reconstructed from council design sessions.
> Includes the approved symbols, typography, color, and print specs.

---

## 1. Primary Brand Symbol — The 11:11 Cross

- The "11:11" wordmark forms a subtle **cross**
- Left 11 and right 11 are subtly differentiated
- Opening and closing marks refined (smaller) after council review
- Used on the **chest (front)**, mid-chest placement
- This is the **primary brand symbol** — always present, always dominant

**Variant: Eye + Clock (Drop 001 foundation imagery)**

- Single abstract minimal eye
- Pupil = clock face, hands at 11:11
- No triangle, no rays, no secondary clock, no text inside
- Line weight: 2–3pt minimum (screen-printable)
- Size: A3 max, centered
- White ink on black tee (culturally neutral)

## 3. Typography

| Use | Typeface | Notes |
|-----|----------|-------|
| Logo wordmark | **Söhne** | Free font; user chose to stick with Söhne after council discussion |
| Back print brand name | **Plus Jakarta Sans Mono** | 14pt |
| Back print spec text | **Plus Jakarta Sans Mono** | 8pt; fallback to Regular if Mono unavailable |
| Body/support text | Plus Jakarta Sans | General |

**Council rationale for Söhne:** The free Söhne font carries the same feel as alternatives
(Plus Jakarta Sans); sticking with Söhne avoids redesigning the logo.

## 4. Color System

| Token | Value | Use |
|-------|-------|-----|
| `base-black` | #0A0A0A | Tee body (primary) |
| `ink-white` | #FFFFFF | Print ink |
| `signal-red` | (discussed) | Accent only, never religious/occult association |

**Rule:** Black tees, white ink. The palette is intentionally minimal —
the brand speaks through symbol, not color.

---

## 5. Design Rules (From Council)

1. **Vector-only** — no photography on prints, no raster images in garments
2. **Max 4 colors** per design (screen print constraint)
3. **2–3pt minimum line weight** — must survive screen printing
4. **A3 max print size** — production cap
5. **No explicit religious iconography** — culturally neutral
6. **11:11 logo stays primary** — back designs cannot overshadow the brand mark
7. **Imperfection is a feature** — the broken circle, the torn wing, the 45° gap
8. **No hand-numbering on label in the design print** — number placement is on
   the label/garment, not the artwork
