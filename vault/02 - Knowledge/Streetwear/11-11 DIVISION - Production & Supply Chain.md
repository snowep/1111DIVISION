## Supplier Notes

- Vendor is **Yogyakarta-based** — the production hub, not the market (Manado)
- MOQ 15 is the vendor minimum → the brand must produce ≥15
- Capital Rp5M limits everything — one production run of 15, no room for rework
- **Printing constraint:** vector only, max 4 colors, A3 max, 2–3pt minimum line weight

---

## Production Risks (From Council)

1. **No rework budget** — a design error at print = 15 unusable shirts
2. **Cultural safety fail = full redesign** — must test with 3 Manado locals first
3. **No photoshoot budget** — content strategy must be mockup/tease-based
4. **Silhouette sourcing:** photograph ambassador (Rp0–200k) is the approved route
5. **Ambassador shirts** — 5 gifted shirts for breakdancers (community seeding)

---

## Print Production Rules

1. Screen print only (no DTG in current scope)
2. Vector artwork only
3. Max 4 colors per design
4. Minimum line weight 2–3pt
5. A3 max print area
6. White ink on black garment (current system)
7. Verified with vendor before full run (sample check recommended)

---

## Vendor Communication Checklist

- [ ] Confirm MOQ 15 and unit price Rp82k
- [ ] Confirm lead time 2–3 weeks
- [ ] Send vector artwork (AI/SVG)
- [ ] Specify print method: screen print, white ink
- [ ] Request sample/print proof before full run
- [ ] Confirm print placement: center back, 10cm below collar, ~25cm tall
