# 11:11 DIVISION — Market & Community Strategy

> The brand's market positioning, target community, and go-to-market approach.
> Reconstructed from the founding council sessions.

---

## Target Market

| Dimension | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Primary** | Breakdancers (B-boys/B-girls) in Manado, North Sulawesi |
| **Secondary** | Indonesian streetwear community; Jakarta for exposure |
| **Psychographic** | Youth in subcultures; faith-adjacent but non-religious messaging |
| **Community need** | Manado talent is strong but under-exposed vs Jakarta |

**The user's motivation:**
> "Why breakdance? I kinda love that culture and want to involve further with them.
> Manado talent is great, but less exposure they've got, Jakarta will..."

The brand exists to **regenerate young talent** in the breakdance community and
give Manado a voice in the Indonesian streetwear scene.

---

## Positioning

- Streetwear with a spiritual undercurrent (never explicit)
- Community-first: the brand is a **vehicle for the breakdance scene**, not just apparel
- Authenticity over hype: organic subculture growth (Stüssy model)
- "We work in the dark to serve the light" as the thesis

---

## Ambassador Strategy

**Original plan (5 gifted shirts):**
- 5 shirts gifted to local breakdancers/ambassadors
- Recipients: breakers, community figures, tastemakers
- Worn organically in the scene → authentic seeding

**Later revision:** The 5-ambassador strategy was questioned; user considered
asking a friend to do a "freeze" (photoshoot pose) instead.

**Final direction:** Photograph the ambassador (council vote B: 6 votes) for
silhouette content — Rp0–200k budget.

---

## Go-To-Market (Drop 001)

1. **Produce 15 pcs** (MOQ) — Yogyakarta vendor
2. **Seed 5 shirts** to ambassadors/breakers
3. **Instagram presence** with lore-gate content (mockup-based, no photoshoot)
4. **Tease before drop** — content without product photos
5. **Drop at 249k max** — price for community, not hype
6. **Cultural safety test** — 3 Manado locals verify no occult association

---

## Instagram Strategy

| Element | Decision |
|---------|----------|
| Handle | @1111division.official |
| Bio | Minimal — no brand name (it's in the handle), no "based in", no vendor info; keep it subculture |
| Avatar | Brand mark / profile design |
| First image | The "gate" — coordinates + lore intro |
| Post style | Mockup-based, text-lore, coordinate tags |
| Post cadence | Tease → drop announcement → product → community |

**Bio rule (user):**
> "For the bio, we don't put our brand name, because that already on the Instagram name,
> we don't put our based, and vendor based, make it more subculture."

---

## Community Principles

1. **Teach without preach** — the brand never lectures
2. **Serve the light in the dark** — community work is quiet, not performative
3. **Support the local scene first** — Manado before Jakarta
4. **Authenticity beats reach** — organic subculture growth, not paid hype
5. **The consumer should feel** — the lore speaks through symbols, not statements
