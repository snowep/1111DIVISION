# DEC-002 — Mission & Motto

## Decision

Adopt the mission **"We work in the dark to serve the light"** and the motto
**"Teach without preach."** The brand's purpose is to regenerate young talent
in the breakdance community and create a street × religion collaboration
without explicitly mentioning Bibles.

## Context

The user defined the brand's soul in the founding pitch: a streetwear brand
that is secretly spiritual. The council refined how to express this without
alienating either subculture or faith communities.

## Rationale

- "Work in the dark" = quiet community work, not performative charity
- "Serve the light" = the spiritual core, never stated directly
- "Teach without preach" = the brand educates by example, not by message

## Options Considered

1. Explicit religious branding (rejected — too alienating)
2. Pure secular streetwear (rejected — loses the soul)
3. **Subtle spiritual undercurrent (accepted)** — the chosen path

## Consequences

- All copywriting follows the "implied, never stated" rule
- The lore carries the spiritual weight, not the branding
- Cultural safety testing is mandatory before any design ships

## Reversal Conditions

- If the user decides to make the faith element explicit, revisit this decision
