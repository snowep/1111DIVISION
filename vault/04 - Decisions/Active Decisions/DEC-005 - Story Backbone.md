# DEC-005 — Story Backbone

## Decision

Adopt the user's story backbone as the canonical lore:

> After the heavenly war, one-third were cast out. Some followed darkness.
> Some regretted it. Some descended by choice to protect humanity.
> All lost their divinity. Broken halos. Torn wings.
> They guide, they tempt, they let humans suffer before helping.
> They teach without preaching. They work in the dark to serve the light.
> **Redemption is a loop, not a finish line.**

## Context

The user wrote this backbone and asked the council whether it still worked
after the design evolved. The council confirmed the backbone remains the
spiritual frame of the brand — it explains the eye, the clock, the loop.

## Rationale

- The eye+clock symbol now has a story to point at
- "Redemption is a loop" matches the 11:11 (loop) and the drop model (chapters)
- The fallen-guardian concept is spiritually resonant without being religious

## Options Considered

1. Replace with a cleaner commercial narrative (rejected — loses soul)
2. Keep the user's original (accepted — canonical)
3. Rewrite from scratch (rejected — unnecessary)

## Consequences

- All drop themes derive from the backbone
- The lore is documented in the brand bible + knowledge base
- Every design choice references the loop / guardians / descent

## Reversal Conditions

- If the user writes a new backbone, this decision is superseded
