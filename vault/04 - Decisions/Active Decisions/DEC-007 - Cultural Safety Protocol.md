# DEC-007 — Cultural Safety Protocol (Manado)

## Decision

Before any Drop 001 design goes to production, it **must** pass a cultural
safety check:

1. Show the design to **3 Manado locals**
2. Test for **occult association** (eye + cross can read as occult)
3. If any local reads it as occult/demonic → **REVERT to topographic halo**
   (pre-approved fallback)
4. Include a **lore card** explaining the meaning:
   "The eye watches. The clock waits. Redemption is a loop."
5. Keep the 11:11 logo as the primary brand symbol front

## Context

The council issued a critical warning: a red cross through the eye has a 90%
occult-association risk in the Manado cultural context. The spiritual theme
must never read as demonic — the brand is faith-adjacent, not occult.

## Rationale

- The brand's mission is "work in the dark to serve the light" — the imagery
  must never invert that meaning
- Manado is the home community; misreading the symbol there is fatal
- The lore card converts a "weird graphic" into "meaning" — context is the defense

## Options Considered

1. Ship as-is (rejected — cultural risk too high)
2. Revert to topographic halo without test (fallback, but loses the story)
3. **Test with 3 locals + lore card (accepted)** — the balanced path

## Consequences

- A lore card must be produced and shipped with Drop 001
- The design is not final until the 3-local test passes
- Future drops inherit this protocol

## Reversal Conditions

- If the 3-local test fails → revert to topographic halo
- If the design is revised → re-run the test
