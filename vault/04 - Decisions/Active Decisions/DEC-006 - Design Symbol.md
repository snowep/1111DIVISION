# DEC-006 — Design Symbol (Eye + Clock)

## Decision

Adopt the **11:11 Cross** as the primary brand symbol (front chest) and the
**Eye + Clock** as the Drop 001 back design — single abstract eye with a clock
pupil at 11:11, no triangle, no rays, no secondary elements.

Locked specs:
- Single eye, abstract, minimal
- Pupil = clock face, hands at 11:11
- No triangle, no rays, no secondary clock
- Line weight 2–3pt minimum (screen-printable)
- A3 max, centered
- White ink on black tee
- Culturally neutral

## Context

The council ran multiple design iterations (topographic halo → breaker
silhouette → eye + clock → combined eye+clock+circle+crosshair → simplified
final). The user locked the single eye + clock as the foundation imagery,
with the 11:11 logo remaining primary.

## Rationale

- The eye = the guardian (watches silently)
- The clock at 11:11 = the loop ("redemption is a loop, not a finish line")
- Minimal line work survives screen printing at 2–3pt
- The subtle cross in the logo remains the brand's identity anchor

## Options Considered

1. Topographic halo (safer, approved earlier — became fallback)
2. Breaker silhouette (denied — cannot produce photography print)
3. Eye + clock + circle + crosshair combined (overcomplicated — simplified)
4. **Single eye + clock (accepted — final)**

## Consequences

- The eye+clock becomes the Drop 001 back graphic
- Must pass Manado cultural safety test (see DEC-007)
- The 11:11 logo remains the front/primary mark
- The symbol must be repeatable across future drops ("10-drop longevity")

## Reversal Conditions

- If the 3-local cultural safety test flags occult association → revert to
  topographic halo (pre-approved fallback)
