# DEC-003 — Drop 001 Product Spec

## Decision

Produce Drop 001 with the following locked specification:

- **Garment:** 16s Australian Coolbreeze Cotton, 235gsm
- **Vendor:** Yogyakarta, Indonesia
- **MOQ:** 15 pcs
- **Unit cost:** ~Rp82,000
- **Retail price:** max Rp249,000 (range 239k–249k)
- **Print:** screen print, white ink, vector-only
- **Lead time:** 2–3 weeks

## Context

The user's capital (Rp5M) and the vendor's MOQ (15) define the starting point.
The council discussed pricing to maximize margin while staying accessible to
the Manado community.

## Rationale

- 235gsm Coolbreeze cotton = quality streetweight, breathable for breakdancing
- 15 pc MOQ fits the capital (Rp1.23M production cost)
- Rp249k max = premium-but-community price; ~67% gross margin
- Yogyakarta vendor = production reality (Manado lacks a vendor)

## Options Considered

1. Higher quantity (rejected — capital insufficient)
2. Lower quality fabric (rejected — brand equity)
3. Jakarta vendor (rejected — cost/quality tradeoff)

## Consequences

- Only 15 pcs exist — scarcity by capital, not marketing
- No rework budget — a print error wastes production entirely
- Every pc must be perfect; quality control is non-negotiable

## Reversal Conditions

- If the vendor cannot deliver at Rp82k/15pcs, renegotiate or find alternative
- If fabric quality changes, re-verify the spec
