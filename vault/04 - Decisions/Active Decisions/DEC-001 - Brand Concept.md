# DEC-001 — Brand Concept

## Decision

Establish **11:11 DIVISION** as a streetwear brand targeting breakdancers in
Manado, Indonesia, with 0 sales, Rp5,000,000 projected capital, and a mission
to regenerate young talent in the breakdance community.

## Context

The user pitched a streetwear brand at founding with 0 sales, 6 Instagram
followers, and Rp5M capital. The brand's purpose: give the Manado breakdance
scene exposure it lacks compared to Jakarta, while creating a subtle
street × religion union — God without explicitly mentioning Bibles.

## Rationale

- Manado has strong breakdance talent but little exposure
- Streetwear is the platform; community is the purpose
- The faith dimension must be implicit, not explicit

## Options Considered

1. Fast-fashion approach (rejected — no capital, no authenticity)
2. Pure local Manado play (accepted as primary)
3. Jakarta-first (rejected — doesn't serve the community mission)

## Consequences

- Production at Yogyakarta vendor, not Manado (supply reality)
- Community-first positioning from day 1
- All messaging carries the "work in the dark to serve the light" ethos

## Reversal Conditions

- If the breakdance community rejects the brand, reposition to broader Indonesian streetwear
- If capital cannot sustain the 15pc MOQ, pause production until funded
